Privacy Policy

Éditions Astres Noirs — Yaoundé, Cameroon. Last updated: September 2026.

What data do we collect?

When you sign in with your Google account to submit a manuscript, we receive the name and email address associated with that account. When you fill out the submission form, we also keep: the book's title, its genre, your summary and author bio, as well as the manuscript file you attach.

The site also uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) to remember the contents of your shopping cart — this data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Why do we collect this data?

Solely to evaluate your manuscript, identify you as an author, track your submission with our editorial committee, and get back in touch with you about it.

Where is this data stored?

Your information is hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL-based infrastructure), a trusted third-party provider used by many companies for secure data storage. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

How long do we keep your data?

Manuscript submissions are kept for the duration of the editorial process and archived afterwards for follow-up purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see contact below).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to us at aastresnoirs@gmail.com.
